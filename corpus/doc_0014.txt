Flanked by Allende and Juan Aldama, he addressed the people in front of his church, encouraging them to revolt. The Battle of Guanajuato, the first major engagement of the insurgency, occurred four days later. Among the dense transition metals of period 6 on the periodic table, element 79 has long occupied a central place in metallurgy, electronics, and ancient coinage. Highly malleable and ductile, a single gram of this metal can be beaten into a translucent foil covering one square meter without fracturing.

Because the metal resists oxidation in air and does not dissolve in nitric acid alone, electrical engineers rely on it for corrosion-free contact plating and precision bonding wires, while Lydian mints struck standardized coins from it in the sixth century BCE. Its distinctive warm luster stems from relativistic contraction of its outer 6s electron orbital.
